=====================================================
   AIM-ASSIST DEMO  --  please play!
=====================================================

This is part of a research project that trains an AI
model to tell HUMAN aim apart from AIM-ASSISTED aim.
Here you get to feel the different assist styles for
yourself.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. You spawn on the T (Terrorist) side with an AK-47.
   The enemies (CT bots) are your targets. Respawn is
   instant -- just keep playing.

2. Play normally  =  you are the HUMAN baseline.

3. HOLD  MOUSE4  =  the AIM ASSIST takes over and
   pulls your crosshair onto the nearest enemy.
   Release MOUSE4 to go back to aiming yourself.

4. Tap  F  to change the assist STYLE:
      RAW  ->  SMOOTH  ->  HUMANISED  ->  HUMANISED-HIGH
   RAW snaps instantly (robotic). Each step looks more
   and more like a real person. Feel the difference!

The current style is shown at the bottom of your
screen at all times.

Have fun -- this is the actual aimbot from the study.
