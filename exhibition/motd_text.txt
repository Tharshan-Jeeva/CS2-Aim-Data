============================================
   CCI Summer Festival - AimTrace Demo
============================================

You spawn T-side with an AK-47. Shoot the
CT bots. Respawn is instant.

  HOLD  V  =  aim assist takes over
  RELEASE  =  you aim (the human)
  TAP   F  =  change assist style:
       raw -> smooth -> humanised -> high

Each style is more human-like: RAW snaps
instantly (obvious robot); HIGH adds reaction
delay, jitter and overshoot so it looks almost
human. Current style shows on-screen.

This is the real aimbot from the study, which
trains an AI to tell human aim from assisted
aim -- can YOU feel the difference?
