============================================
   AIM-ASSIST DEMO  --  please play!
============================================

You spawn T-side with an AK-47. Shoot the
CT bots. Respawn is instant.

  HOLD  V  =  aim assist takes over
  RELEASE  =  you aim (the human)
  TAP   F  =  change assist style:
       raw -> smooth -> humanised -> high

Each style is more human-like than the last:
RAW snaps instantly (obvious robot), while
HIGH adds reaction delay, jitter and overshoot
so it looks almost human. The current style
shows at the bottom of your screen.

This is the real aimbot from the study. The
project trains an AI to tell human aim from
assisted aim -- so see if YOU can feel the
difference. Have fun!
