# 🎯 Can YOU spot the aimbot?

### Play Counter-Strike — and feel an AI aim-assist take over your mouse.

This demo is part of a research project that trains an AI model to tell
**human aim** apart from **aim-assisted aim**. Try it yourself 👇

---

## How to play

**1.  You spawn with an AK-47.**  Shoot the enemy bots. You respawn instantly —
just keep going.

**2.  Play normally  =  you're the HUMAN.**

**3.  HOLD `MOUSE4`  =  the AIM ASSIST takes over**
and snaps your crosshair onto the nearest enemy.
Let go to aim yourself again.

**4.  Tap `F`  =  change the assist style:**

```
   RAW  →  SMOOTH  →  HUMANISED  →  HUMANISED-HIGH
  robotic ─────────────────────────► human-like
```

> The current style is shown at the **bottom of the screen**.

---

### 👀 Feel the difference

- **RAW** snaps instantly — obviously a machine.
- **SMOOTH** glides on.
- **HUMANISED / HIGH** add reaction delay, jitter, overshoot and drift —
  it starts to look like a real person.

**That's the whole challenge of the project:** as the assist gets more
human-like, can a machine still tell it's not you?
